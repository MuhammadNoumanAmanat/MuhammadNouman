# Muhammad Nouman Amanat

**Research Associate | Electrical Engineering Graduate | Edge AI & Signal Processing**

I am an electrical engineering graduate from **Information Technology University of the Punjab (ITU), Lahore**, currently working as a **Research Associate at RESOLVE Lab, UET Lahore**. My work focuses on developing efficient machine-learning and signal-processing systems for resource-constrained hardware and real-world sensing applications.

My interests include **Edge AI, self-supervised representation learning, efficient inference, embedded systems, multimodal sensing, and signal processing**. I am particularly interested in graduate research opportunities involving hardware-aware deep learning and intelligent sensing systems.

## Bachelor's thesis (2025)

**[Wearable Smart Insole for Early Diabetic Foot Ulcer Prediction Using Multi-Modal Sensing](bachelors-thesis/README.md)** — BSc Electrical Engineering final-year research project at ITU, Lahore. The project combines multimodal wearable sensing, Arduino-based data acquisition, MATLAB visualization and LSTM-based time-series analysis.
